^IJKL....^ Forward/backward/turn
^Space...^ Fire

Walk into doors to open them.
Walk into secret walls to push them.
Walk over items to pick them up.

If you have a mouse in port 1 and
you have enabled it in the Options,
you can turn and shoot with it.

If you have a joystick in port 2 and
you have enabled it in the Options,
you can move and shoot with it.
With a two button joystick, holding
down the second button allows you
to strafe.
